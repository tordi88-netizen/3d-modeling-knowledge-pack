# Organic Workflow

## Recommended order
1. Reference and anatomy breakdown
2. Low-res blockout
3. Sculpt primary forms
4. Refine landmarks
5. Secondary forms
6. Retopology if needed
7. UVs
8. Texture, groom, rig, or pose

## Notes
- Read anatomy as layered forms, not lines.
- Do not jump to pores or wrinkles too early.
- Strong planes matter more than micro detail.
- Asymmetry helps realism after the main structure is correct.
