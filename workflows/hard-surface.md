# Hard Surface Workflow

## Recommended order
1. Gather reference
2. Primitive blockout
3. Refine large forms
4. Boolean or kitbash for exploration
5. Clean supporting edges
6. Bevel/chamfer pass
7. UVs and baking
8. Texture and presentation

## Notes
- Keep bevel widths visually consistent.
- Large surfaces need subtle variation to avoid looking sterile.
- Use weighted normals or face-weighted normals where appropriate.
- Separate design exploration from final topology pass.
