# ZBrush Notes

## Best use cases
- Organic sculpting
- Concept sculpting
- High-frequency detail
- Exploratory shape work

## Workflow notes
- Stay low subdivision as long as possible.
- Use subdivision levels strategically.
- Polish primary forms before surface noise.
- Retopologize outside or inside ZBrush depending on the target.
- Keep export scale and naming consistent when round-tripping.
