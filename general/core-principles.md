# Core Modeling Principles

## Main goals
- Preserve readable forms before adding detail.
- Work from large shapes to medium forms to small details.
- Keep silhouettes strong.
- Use clean naming and scene organization.
- Favor non-destructive workflows when possible.

## Common priorities
1. Proportion
2. Silhouette
3. Primary forms
4. Secondary forms
5. Surface detail

## Good habits
- Block out early and iterate fast.
- Check the model from multiple camera angles.
- Compare against reference constantly.
- Save milestone versions.
- Separate design problems from topology cleanup.
