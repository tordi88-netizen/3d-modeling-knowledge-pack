# Topology Guide

## General rules
- Keep topology clean enough for the task, not cleaner than needed.
- Use quads where deformation or subdivision matters.
- Triangles are acceptable in static or flat areas when controlled.
- Avoid long thin polygons in deforming regions.
- Redirect edge flow intentionally around eyes, mouth, shoulders, and joints.

## Subdivision-friendly topology
- Maintain even polygon density.
- Support sharp forms with control loops rather than random creases.
- Place poles where they create minimal shading or deformation issues.

## When perfection is unnecessary
- Game-ready meshes often need optimization over purity.
- Boolean-heavy hard-surface meshes can be cleaned after design lock.
